Assumptions made where prompt 05 did not settle the point
==============================================================================

 1. 'Sessions that included a low' = low_during == 1 or low_post2h == 1, which
    is exactly low_any == 1, so the two groups partition the usable explore set.
 2. Session start/end are the 5-min grid rows in sessions.csv (overrides
    applied); 'start minus 30 min' is the row six steps before start.
 3. Search windows: pump cut and glucose fall are searched up to end + 2 h
    (the outcome span, so a late cut after the session still counts); HR rise
    and steps rise are searched up to the session END, because a rise after
    the workout is not the body signalling exercise. A signal not found in its
    window is NaN and that session drops out of any gap using it.
 4. Pump cut: both rows t and t + 5 need frac_suspend >= 0.5 and the six rows
    t - 30 .. t - 5 all need frac_suspend < 0.5. If any of those rows is off
    the grid (window edge) the condition is treated as not met. A pump that
    was already suspended at start - 30 min has no 'first cut' unless it
    resumes for 30 min and cuts again; the count of such sessions is printed,
    and cut_before_window (suspend run already running at start - 35 and
    start - 30) marks sessions whose recorded cut is that later one. The gap
    tables are repeated without them as a sensitivity check.
 5. HR baseline = median hr_mean over [start - 150, start - 30) min, rows with
    hr_missing excluded; hr_mean is the per-interval mean from build_dataset.
    A rise needs hr_mean >= baseline + 30 on a row with HR present.
 6. Steps rise uses the aligned 'steps' column (sum of watch step records in
    the interval), threshold 200 in one 5-min row.
 7. Glucose fall = glucose(t) - glucose(t - 15 min) <= -0.5, first row at or
    after start; rows with either reading missing are skipped, not
    interpolated. This uses the aligned union glucose, not slope30 / slope15
    from sessions.csv.
 8. Quartiles are pandas linear-interpolation quantiles over the sessions
    where the signal (or both signals of a gap) is present; n is given for
    every cell. Gaps are signed: positive = the first-named event is later.
 9. g0 bands use g0 from sessions.csv (last reading in the 15 min up to
    start): < 7.0, 7.0 to 9.9 (i.e. 7.0 <= g0 < 10.0), >= 10.0.
10. Activity mode: activity_at_start and activity_lead_min are taken from
    sessions.csv unchanged (lead = consecutive rows with frac_activity > 0
    immediately before start).
11. The sanity print picks, among sessions with a low where all five signals
    are present, the middle session of each cat2, then fills to five in
    session order.
12. No model is fitted; no prospective row is read past the initial filter.
