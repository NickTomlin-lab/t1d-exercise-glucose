==============================================================================
Assumptions made where prompt 03 did not settle the point
==============================================================================

 1. Pooled table = usable explore sessions (usable == 1, session_split ==
    explore) plus every control window in control_windows.csv that belongs to
    one of them. Controls are already explore-only by construction.
 2. Rows with any missing value among g0, slope30, bolus_iob_u,
    est_basal_iob_u, insulin_total_4h, mins_since_meal are dropped
    individually (a session's controls are kept even if the session itself
    were dropped, and vice versa). Control weights use k = controls REMAINING
    in the pooled table, so each set's controls still sum to weight 1.
 3. pooled_windows.csv carries two extra columns beyond the requested list:
    weight and fold_month (calendar month of the session start).
 4. Fold = calendar month of the SESSION start (fold_month = months since
    [month], so Dec = 0 and the 2026 months keep their calendar number);
    controls are assigned the month of their session, not their own date (a
    control up to 28 days from its session can therefore sit in a different
    calendar month from its own start). Training = every set with a strictly
    earlier fold month. [month] sets are training-only. April holds only 13 days of window 1 and no
    sessions fall in the [date] gap.
 5. Standardisation: unweighted mean/sd of the training rows; a predictor
    that is constant in the training rows (e.g. cricket_outdoor before May)
    is left at 0 after centring, so it contributes nothing in that fold.
    Indicator columns and the interaction are standardised like the others.
 6. Weighted logistic regression is scikit-learn's: 0.5 * ||beta||^2 +
    C * sum_i w_i * logloss_i, intercept unpenalised, lbfgs. B0 is the
    weighted base rate of the training rows.
 7. Weighted Brier / log loss = sum(w * loss) / sum(w); AUC is
    roc_auc_score with sample_weight. n is given as rows, sets and sum of
    weights. Log loss clips probabilities to [1e-9, 1 - 1e-9].
 8. Paired Brier difference: cluster bootstrap resamples set_id from the
    pooled out-of-fold predictions (no refitting), 2,000 draws, seed
    20260921, percentile interval; the same resampled sets are used for
    every comparison in a table. A training fold with a single outcome class
    would fall back to the base rate and be noted; none occurred unless a
    note is printed above.
 9. Calibration tertiles are unweighted terciles of the out-of-fold
    predicted probability; mean predicted and observed rate in each bin are
    weight-averaged (unweighted observed rate also shown).
10. Odds ratios are on the raw scale: standardised coefficient divided by
    the training sd of that column, exponentiated. For M_int the 'exercise'
    row is the exercise effect at insulin_total_4h = 0; an extra row gives
    the exercise OR at the pooled median insulin_total_4h.
11. OR bootstrap: resample set_id with replacement, refit the same model
    (standardising on the resample), 2,000 draws, seed 20260921, percentile
    interval of the raw-scale coefficient. Draws in which a column is
    constant give NaN for that term (boot_valid counts the rest); draws
    with a single outcome class are skipped and counted.
12. Part 5a re-runs the full model list; Part 5b runs B1, M_ins4, M_full
    only, as asked. Each bootstrap block restarts the generator from the
    same seed so every table is reproducible on its own.
